# 11. Profile requests, grievances and notices

[← Handbook](README.md) · [Field Portal guide](04-employee-field-portal.md) · [Employee records and documents](07-employee-records-and-documents.md)

These features are for an employee whose Field Portal login is **linked to an employee record**. A linked Field Officer can use them for their own record too. Use your own account and an internet connection. An unlinked Field Officer can perform permitted field work but cannot use an employee's personal requests or inbox. Client Portal users cannot use these features.

## Ask HR to correct your profile

Open **Menu → My profile → Request profile changes**. This sends a request; it does not change your record immediately.

1. Choose the fields that are wrong and enter each **New value**. The screen shows the current value for comparison. You can request changes to your name, birth date, gender, guardian details, mobile/email, addresses, Aadhaar/PAN, UAN/ESIC, or bank details.
2. For a name, birth date, Aadhaar, PAN, or bank change, choose a matching **Supporting document version** that is already uploaded under **My documents**. Upload the document there first if the list is empty. UAN/ESIC evidence is optional. An uploaded document is evidence for HR review; **Received** or **Staff-reviewed** does not mean KYC-verified.
3. Add a short note if it helps HR understand the correction, then tap **Send to HR**. Do not send a second request while one is pending. Check **Request history → View** for each field's result and HR's reason.

HR may approve some fields and reject others. An approved bank change creates a new current bank-account version; older versions remain. If your record changed while HR was reviewing, HR must refresh instead of overwriting the newer value. You may **Withdraw** the undecided part of a pending request; changes already approved stay in place. If the request says **Resubmission required** after a company transfer, make a new request in your current company with appropriate documents. Pay, employment type, role and duty assignment changes are handled by staff outside this form.

**HR/Admin:** Open **Profile requests** in the correct company, select a request, and compare the original, current and proposed value for each field. Open its supporting document if required. Approve or reject fields with the required reasons. Bank details are decided as one group. A staff member cannot approve their own request. The list is a summary; open the request only when your job requires seeing its private values. Do not copy full ID or bank numbers into comments, screenshots or support messages. A request from the employee's previous company does not move into the new company's HR queue.

## Raise and follow a grievance

Open **Menu → Grievances**. Enter a clear **Subject** and **Describe your concern**, then tap **Send to HR**. This is an identifiable case, not an anonymous message. Do not include passwords or unnecessary ID/bank numbers in the text. Your company's HR team receives the case.

Your **case history** shows HR replies, who handled the case, and the recorded resolution. Open a case to **Send reply** while it is open. If HR marked it **Resolved** but the issue continues, enter why and tap **Reopen case**. The previous resolution remains in the history.

HR aims to resolve each open case within **24 elapsed hours** of submission or reopening. **Due soon** means the last four hours of that period; **Overdue** means the target time has passed. These labels show timing, not that the case was automatically escalated or resolved. Reopening begins a new 24-hour period. If your case is urgent or involves immediate safety, use HHCIL's direct emergency or supervisor process too; the in-app case does not send a push alert.

**HR/Admin:** In the selected company, open **Grievance desk**. Filter **Open**, **Resolved**, or **All**; open a case to review its event history. Assign an authorized HR handler where needed, **Send reply**, and **Resolve case** with a reason. Watch the due time and **Due soon/Overdue** label. A staff member cannot handle their own case. After an employee transfers, the old company's HR keeps its case; the destination company's HR cannot read it merely because the employee moved. The employee keeps access to their own history.

## Read notices in the Field Portal

Tap the **bell** at the top of the Field Portal. Its number counts published notices you have not opened. Tap a notice to read it; the first time you open it is recorded. Tap **Refresh** in the inbox to check for updates. Notices need an internet connection to load. They are in-app messages: the system records when one became available and when you opened it, **not** whether your phone received a push message or whether you understood the content.

A notice addressed to you stays in your inbox history after a site or company transfer. A **withdrawn** notice stays listed but its old text is hidden and it no longer counts as unread. HR may publish a replacement notice to correct it. If a notice appears wrong or is missing, tell HR the notice title and your employee code; do not use a coworker's login.

**HR/Admin:** Open **Notices** in the Admin Portal and check the selected company. Choose **All employees**, **Selected sites**, or **Named employees**. Search and select sites or employees when needed. A Super Administrator may select several companies; HR may publish only for the selected company.

1. Enter a short title and the notice text. Choose a withdrawn notice in **Replaces withdrawn notice** only when correcting an earlier publication.
2. Tap **Preview audience**. Check the number of recipients for each company and the sample names. This is a sample, so verify the chosen companies, sites and employee selection as well. Confirm the review checkbox, then tap **Publish notice**. If membership changed after preview, preview again before publishing.
3. Open **Published in this company → View** to see the saved notice, recipient count and how many recipients opened it. Publication freezes the recipient list; later transfers do not add or remove recipients from that notice.
4. Published text cannot be edited. To remove a mistake, open the notice, enter a reason and choose **Withdraw notice**. Then publish a corrected replacement if needed. A multi-company notice can be withdrawn only by a Super Administrator.

Notices do not send email, SMS or push notifications in this release. Use HHCIL's other approved communication channels for urgent instructions. A notice being **available** is not proof that every employee saw it.

## If something does not work

- **Request cannot be sent:** check internet, required fields and document evidence. If another profile request is pending, wait for HR or withdraw its undecided part before submitting a new one.
- **HR cannot approve:** the employee value or document may have changed, the evidence may have been deleted, or the request may belong to another company. Refresh and review the latest record; do not bypass the conflict.
- **Grievance or inbox is missing:** check that your Field login is linked to your own active employee record. An unlinked Field Officer has no personal ESS inbox. Ask HR to check the account link and permissions.
- **Notice audience changed:** HR should preview again and confirm the new count before publishing. A withdrawn notice's text cannot be restored by the employee; HR publishes a corrected replacement.

See [Common Errors & Solutions](08-common-errors-and-solutions.md) for more recovery steps. Give HR the page, selected company, employee code, approximate time and the visible error. Do not send a password or full identity/bank number in a support message.
