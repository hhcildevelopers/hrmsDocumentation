# Leave setup (C1 preview)

The Leave screen is an early setup preview. **Employees cannot request leave or see a balance yet.** HR must first approve the rules for each company; this handbook will be updated when requests and approvals are released.

## For HR and Super Administrators

1. Open **Common → Leave policies**. The HHCIL office example shows 18 privilege/earned leave days, 7 casual leave days and 7 sick leave days, but it is only a **draft**. Review all undecided rules with HHCIL HR/legal. A blank rule is not zero.
2. Save a new version when you make changes. Earlier versions stay available as history. Record sign-off only after the responsible people have approved the complete rules. Then use **Apply** to choose the companies and effective date. A policy can be shared by several companies, or a company can use a different policy. Saving a draft does not apply it.
3. Open **Common → Holidays** in the selected company. Add a holiday for all of that company's sites or just one site. A site's weekly offs come from the employee's assigned working weekdays; they are not entered as holidays.
4. Open **Employees → employee details → Personal → Leave & field classification**. Tick **Internal employee** for eligible office staff; this also turns on **Leave applicable**. For another eligible employee, such as a Field Officer, tick only **Leave applicable** as appropriate. Enter a reason and save. Do not tick it for a guard who is not eligible. The **Field Officer** checkbox is a classification, not a new login role or permission.
5. Choose the employee's correct office shift (09:30–18:00 or 10:00–18:30, if applicable) in the existing assignment screen. Do not choose it based on gender. If the employee transfers to another company, destination HR must review their leave classification again.

If you cannot edit a policy, ask a Super Administrator for the specific Common Leave Policy permission. HR users can review policies by default, while editing or signing off requires an explicit grant. Employee classification also requires employee-edit permission.

## For employees

If HR has marked you leave-applicable, the Field Portal menu shows **Leave**. You can see your company's applied policy, if one is active, plus upcoming holidays and weekly offs. The listed CL/SL/PL days describe a policy's annual amounts; **they are not your current balance**. If the policy is not active yet, the page explains that HR is still completing setup. If you believe you should have the Leave menu but do not, ask HR to check your employee record and company.

You still punch according to your current duty. The Field Officer checkbox does **not** remove the site's GPS requirement in C1. Late punch-in reasons and field-location rules are planned for a later checkpoint.
