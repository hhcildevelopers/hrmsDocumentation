# Attendance reports

The **Reports → Attendance** page lets an HR Administrator or Super Administrator view a company's attendance muster and save a report version. Select the correct company at the top of the Admin Portal first.

1. Choose a site or **All sites in selected company**.
2. Choose a period: calendar month, **21 to 20**, **26 to 25**, or **Custom dates**. Custom dates may cover 1 to 31 consecutive days. For a 21-to-20 report ending in October, choose October as the ending month; the report covers 21 September through 20 October.
3. Add a Payment Date only if needed for the report heading. This does not mean anyone was paid.
4. Click **Preview**. Scroll the table sideways to see all dates and totals. A `?` marks a day that needs review, and the reason appears below that site's table. Correct the source record in Attendance review or Leave requests, then preview again.
5. Once the period has ended and no days are unresolved, click **Finalize**. Use the saved version's **PDF** or **Excel** buttons to download the same data in either format.
6. If source attendance changes later, open the saved report and **Reopen** it with a reason. Preview again and finalize a new numbered version. The older version remains available for audit.

Common codes: `P` attended, `PW` worked an approved weekly off, `HD` half-day due to unresolved or rejected late approval, `WO` weekly off, `H` approved holiday without attendance or leave, `AB` absence explicitly marked by HR, `CO` compensatory off, and `CL`/`SL`/`PL` approved leave. A missing punch does not automatically mean absence. Hours use the effective times after any authorized correction; a manual correction is **not GPS verified**. These codes and hours do not calculate wages.

This report is in development review. HR must approve the generated PDF layout and reconciliation rules before it is used for payroll or shared with clients.
